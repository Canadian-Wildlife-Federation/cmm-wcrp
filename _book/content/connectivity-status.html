Current Connectivity Status

In the Maqmekwitk watershed, 218.23 km of Plamu habitat are currently connected to the ocean and 266.67 km are disconnected from it. This means that 45.01% of the 484.90 km of total habitat is connected.

In the Maqmekwitk watershed, 191.80 km of Kataq habitat are currently connected to the ocean and 216.67 km are disconnected from it. This means that 46.96% of the 408.46 km of total habitat is connected.

Structure Count

In the Maqmekwitk watershed, 515 structures potentially disconnect Plamu habitat. Of these, 8 are identified as barriers in need of rehabilitation (priority barriers), 0 are identified as barriers that do not warrant rehabilitation (non-actionable), and 505 require further field assessment.

In the Maqmekwitk watershed, 176 structures potentially disconnect Kataq habitat. Of these, 8 are identified as barriers in need of rehabilitation (priority barriers), 0 are identified as barriers that do not warrant rehabilitation (non-actionable), and 167 require further field assessment.
